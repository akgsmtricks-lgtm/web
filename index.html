High-Precision Mechanical Drafting & Product Development

Optimizing part geometry, tooling feasibility, and material choice before cutting metal.

ApexMech operates as an integrated mechanical design house for production plants, equipment builders, fabricators, and automotive suppliers across India and international markets. Our engineers take full ownership of complex mechanical projects, moving from rough concepts, legacy 2D blueprints, or physical parts directly to validated SolidWorks and Inventor CAD assemblies.

By strictly implementing Design for Manufacturing and Assembly (DFM/DFA) rules, our drafting team eliminates on-site assembly interferences, ensures correct sheet metal K-factors, and prepares unambiguous GD&T blueprints that machinists can immediately execute.
