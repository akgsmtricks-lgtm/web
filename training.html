Hands-On Portfolio Requirements

Industry Capstone Projects

Every trainee independently completes and documents these 5 industrial projects under engineer mentorship.

PROJECT 01

BIW Hood Panel Design Using Master-Section

Developing the outer panel, inner reinforcement panel with embossments, latch/striker reinforcement, and hinge assembly based on OEM master-sections.

PROJECT 02

Plastic Door Trim Assembly Design

Creating injection-molded automotive door cards complete with dog houses, heat stakes, snaps, push clips, and tooling draft verification.

PROJECT 03

Electrical Harness Layout

Routing a multi-branch engine bay wiring bundle in 3D CAD, defining sheet metal retaining clips, and generating flattened 2D manufacturing drawings.

PROJECT 04

Aerodynamic Study of Vehicle Body

Executing virtual wind-tunnel flow simulation over a sedan/SUV body to identify wake separation regions, vortex paths, and optimize coefficient of drag (Cd).

PROJECT 05

Production Drawings for Automotive Assemblies

Producing complete ASME Y14.5M compliant 2D workshop manufacturing blueprints with comprehensive GD&T feature control frames, surface finish callouts, and indented Bill of Materials (BOM).
